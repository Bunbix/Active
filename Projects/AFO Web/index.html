My Professional Journey

Olabisi Afolabi is an accomplished accountant with over ten years of diverse experience in public accounting. His expertise encompasses ledger processes, account reconciliations, and streamlining accounting operations. Originally from Nigeria, where he worked with several accounting firms, Olabisi relocated with his family to Gilbert Plains, Manitoba, Canada, in 2014 as a skilled worker. He holds a Higher National Diploma in Accountancy (2004) from Osun State College of Technology in Nigeria and is a member of the National Association of Certified Public Bookkeepers (NACPB). To further specialize in the accounting industry, Olabisi is currently pursuing his Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) designation. Olabisi began his career in public accounting as an Audit Assistant at Gbenga Akinwale & Co. Chartered Accountants (2004–2006) and later at Tunji Labode & Co. Certified National Accountants. After moving to Canada, he built a robust career, serving as a Senior Accounting Technician at Goossen Accounting Ltd. for five years. He then joined Accent Chartered Professional Accountants in 2017, where he was a valued team member for two years. From 2019, he contributed his skills at ATMOS Financial Services in a part-time capacity for four years. Currently, Olabisi applies his extensive experience as the Managing Partner of Olabisi Afolabi Accounting & Co. He is a flexible professional who adapts seamlessly to evolving accounting processes and technology. Olabisi is adept at financial reporting and analysis for both the private and public sectors. He is passionate about providing comprehensive accounting services to individuals, businesses, and government clients, including tax preparation, government correspondence, financial statement preparation, general ledger accounting, and bank reconciliations. Outside of his professional life, Olabisi is happily married to Catherine, and they are blessed with three children.
