About Olabisi Afolabi

With extensive experience in accounting and bookkeeping management, Olabisi Afolabi established AFOLABI ACCOUNTING & CO. to provide personalized, reliable accounting services to individuals and businesses across Canada.

Located in Gilbert Plains, Manitoba, we're committed to helping our clients achieve financial clarity, confidence, and sustainable growth through expert guidance and professional service.

Professional Qualifications

Higher National Diploma in Accountancy (2004) from Osun State College of Technology

Professional Memberships

National Association of Certified Public Bookkeepers (NACPB)

Expertise

QuickBooks, tax planning, financial analysis, and modern accounting technology

Professional Journey

Olabisi is an accomplished accountant with over 10 years of diverse experience in public accounting, including:

  • Ledger processes and account reconciliation
  • Financial reporting and analysis
  • Tax compliance and planning
  • Business advisory services
  • Technology integration and training

Currently: Managing Partner of Afolabi Accounting & Co., serving clients across Canada.

Passion: Combining traditional accounting expertise with modern financial technology to deliver exceptional value to clients.
